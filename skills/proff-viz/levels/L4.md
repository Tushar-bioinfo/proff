- **Never ask the reader to picture it or to already know it.** If an idea has a shape, a count, or a step, draw it or compute it. Do not write "imagine", "picture this", "clearly", "obviously", "intuitively", or "as you know"; the audit flags them.
- **No idea before its step.** List the smaller ideas the target needs, down to one the reader can check by counting. Each must appear on the page before it is used: a tiny example with real numbers first, then the general form. A symbol appears only after the example it names.
- The order is yours: climb from the smallest rung, open on a puzzle and take it apart, follow one unit through, and so on (`ref/styles.md`, story arcs). Pick the arc that fits this question.
