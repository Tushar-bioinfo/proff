Examples, not limits. Invent the visual that makes this idea land, and cover every important part.

## What to show
- Mechanism over time → moving parts, then binding or activation. Compare before/after; label the causal step.
- Zoom levels → tissue, region, object, molecule. Keep scale bars and mark the region carried between views.
- Assay → physical measurement beside inferred quantity. Mark assumptions and what the instrument cannot measure.
- Pathway → overview, then stage zoom-ins with an inhibitor. Compare treated/control; label the blocked action.
- Genome → aligned tracks sharing coordinates and units. Mark strands, regions, and measured versus inferred signals.
- Spatial assay → spots over tissue, then cells within spots. Compare mixtures and resolved cells; label resolution limits.
- Controls → positive, negative, and experimental panels on the same scale. Annotate the expected signal and alternative explanation.
